2. The service

JitsFlow is a no-gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu study app (free for students) and software that gyms use to run their timetable, bookings, members, fees and payment reminders. It's a digital service: there are no physical goods and nothing is shipped. You get access as soon as you sign up, and a gym's plan applies as soon as it's set up.

3. Accounts

  • Give accurate details, keep your password to yourself, and tell us if you think someone else has used your account.
  • If you're under 18, you need a parent's or guardian's permission to create an account. Gyms that add children must have a parent's permission first (see the privacy notice).
  • A coach who sets up a gym confirms they're allowed to act for that gym, and accepts these terms for it.

4. Gym plans and billing

  • Free months: a new gym's first 3 months are free. No card is needed to start, and nothing is charged unless the head coach adds a card.
  • Price: after the free months, R99 a month per gym, in South African rand. Prices are in South African rand. We're not VAT registered, so no VAT is added. Gyms accepted into the JitsFlow beta get 6 months free, then R49 a month while they stay in the beta.
  • Recurring billing: when you add a card, you authorise a monthly charge of the plan price through Payfast, until you cancel. The first charge is on the day after your free months end (the app shows this date), or on the day you add the card if they've already ended, and then on about the same date every month. There's no fixed term and no contract.
  • Failed payments: if a payment doesn't go through, the app asks the head coach to add a card again. We don't lock your gym out while it's sorted.
  • Price changes: we'll give you at least 30 days' notice by email before a new price applies to your plan. If you don't want to continue at the new price, cancel before it applies.
  • Cancelling: cancel any time in the app: Gym tab › Your JitsFlow plan › Cancel plan, or email us. Payfast won't charge your card again. Your gym keeps its access at least until the end of the month you've paid for, and its data stays until you delete the gym. See the refund and cancellation policy.

5. Member payments

  • Members pay their gym, not JitsFlow. The gym is the merchant (the seller) for its memberships, class packs and other fees. The gym sets its prices and its own membership terms.
  • Members pay their gym by EFT with their own reference today. Card, Apple Pay and Capitec Pay are coming soon: card payments will be processed by Paystack and paid by Paystack directly into the gym's bank account, so JitsFlow never holds membership money.   • JitsFlow provides the software: the Pay sheet, reminders and records. We aren't a party to the agreement between a member and their gym.

  • A gym that takes card payments gives its bank account in the app and confirms it's the gym's own account. We check it before card payments switch on, and Paystack may hold the first payout to a new or changed account until it's verified.
  • Questions or disputes about membership fees, refunds, classes or cancellations are between the member and the gym. We'll help where we can: email us.

9. Train safely

Jiu-Jitsu is a contact sport with a real risk of injury. JitsFlow is a study aid: it doesn't replace a qualified coach and isn't medical advice. Learn and drill techniques under a qualified coach, tap early, release submissions when your partner taps, and see a doctor about injuries. You train at your own risk.
