How billing works

  1. Start free. Start your gym with no card. Your first 3 months are free.
  2. Add a card when you're ready. In the app: Gym tab › Your JitsFlow plan › Add a card. You enter your card on Payfast's secure checkout. Nothing is charged during your free months.
  3. Monthly billing. Payfast charges R99 on the day after your free months end, then on about the same date each month. The app shows the date your free months end. If you add a card after that, the first charge is on the day you add it.
  4. Cancel any time. Gym tab › Your JitsFlow plan › Cancel plan. Payfast won't charge your card again. There's no cancellation fee and no notice period.

If you don't add a card, nothing is charged and nothing locks: the app reminds the head coach that the free months are over. After you cancel, your gym and its data stay in JitsFlow, and you can add a card again whenever you like. We don't refund part-months; see the refund policy. If we ever change the price, we'll tell you by email at least 30 days before the new price applies.

Member payments

Members pay their gym by EFT with their own reference today. Card, Apple Pay and Capitec Pay are coming soon: card payments will be processed by Paystack and paid by Paystack directly into the gym's bank account, so JitsFlow never holds membership money. JitsFlow's 2% platform fee and Paystack's processing fee will be deducted from each card payment.Members pay their gym by card, Apple Pay, Capitec Pay or EFT. Card payments are processed by Paystack and paid by Paystack directly into the gym's bank account; JitsFlow never holds membership money. JitsFlow's 2% platform fee and Paystack's processing fee are deducted from each card payment.

The gym is the seller of its memberships, and JitsFlow is the software in between. The gym doesn't need a payment account of its own: it gives its bank account in the app once, and we check it before card payments switch on.

  • EFT with a reference: free. Members pay the gym's bank account with their own reference and send a proof of payment in the app.
  • Card, Apple Pay and Capitec Pay: coming soon, on Paystack's secure checkout.on Paystack's secure checkout (Apple Pay on devices that support it). Paystack pays the gym's share into the gym's bank account, normally 2 working days after each payment.
  • JitsFlow's 2% platform fee is split off by Paystack at the time of each card payment. The member pays the gym's normal price; the fee comes out of what the gym receives, and so does Paystack's processing fee (see paystack.com/za/pricing).

JitsFlow's own gym plan is billed separately, through Payfast (above).
