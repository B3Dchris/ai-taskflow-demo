Pricing

Simple pricing

How billing works

  1. Start free. Start your gym with no card. Your first 3 months are free.
  2. Add a card when you're ready. In the app: Gym tab › Your JitsFlow plan › Add a card. You enter your card on Payfast's secure checkout. Nothing is charged during your free months.
  3. Monthly billing. Payfast charges R99 on the day after your free months end, then on about the same date each month. The app shows the date your free months end. If you add a card after that, the first charge is on the day you add it.
  4. Cancel any time. Gym tab › Your JitsFlow plan › Cancel plan. Payfast won't charge your card again. There's no cancellation fee and no notice period.

If you don't add a card, nothing is charged and nothing locks: the app reminds the head coach that the free months are over. After you cancel, your gym and its data stay in JitsFlow, and you can add a card again whenever you like. We don't refund part-months; see the refund policy. If we ever change the price, we'll tell you by email at least 30 days before the new price applies.

Member payments

Each gym connects its own Payfast account, so members pay the gym directly and the gym is the seller of its memberships. JitsFlow is the software in between.

  • EFT with a reference: free. Members pay the gym's bank account with their own reference and send a proof of payment in the app.
  • Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay: coming soon. When a gym switches them on, payments go through Payfast's secure checkout into the gym's Payfast account.
  • JitsFlow's 2% platform fee is charged on member payments processed through the gym's Payfast account. Payfast splits it off at the time of payment (Payfast Split Payments). The member pays the gym's normal price; the fee comes out of what the gym receives. Payfast's own transaction fees are charged to the gym by Payfast.
