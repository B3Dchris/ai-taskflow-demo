Refunds and cancellation

Refund and cancellation policy

Cancel your gym's plan any time and you won't be charged again. If we charged you by mistake, we refund it in full.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Your gym's JitsFlow plan

  • Cancel any time in the app: Gym tab › Your JitsFlow plan › Cancel plan, or email support@jitsflow.com. There's no cancellation fee and no notice period, and Payfast won't charge your card again.
  • After you cancel, your gym keeps its access at least until the end of the month you've paid for. Its data stays until you delete the gym, and you can add a card again any time.
  • No part-month refunds: the monthly fee covers the month it's charged for, so we don't refund the unused part of a month.
  • Billing errors and duplicate charges are refunded in full, to the card you paid with, through Payfast. Refunds usually reach your account within 7 to 10 business days after we confirm the error, depending on your bank.
  • Where ECTA's 7-day cooling-off right applies to you, you can cancel within 7 days of the agreement and get a full refund.

How to ask for a refund

Email support@jitsflow.com with your gym's name, the date and amount of the charge, and what went wrong. We'll check it with Payfast and confirm by email, and the refund goes back to the card that was charged. Refunds are never more than the original payment, and never to a different card.

Membership fees you pay your gym

  • Your gym is the seller of its memberships, so refunds of membership fees, class packs and other fees are up to your gym and its own terms. Contact your gym first.
  • A gym refunds a card payment in the app (Money), in full or in part, back to the card it was paid with, through Paystack. Paystack reverses the payment's split (the gym's share and JitsFlow's 2% platform fee) under its own refund rules.
  • If you can't sort it out with your gym, email us and we'll help where we can.

Chargebacks

Please contact us (for your gym's plan) or your gym (for membership fees) before disputing a card payment with your bank. Most problems are quicker to fix directly. If a chargeback is raised, it's handled under the card schemes' rules by Payfast (for a gym's plan) or Paystack (for membership fees). For membership fees the gym gives its evidence, which we pass to Paystack, and we give the processor the records it asks for.

Delivery

JitsFlow is a digital service. Access is immediate after you sign up or pay, and there are no physical goods, so nothing is shipped and there are no delivery fees. Prices are in South African rand, with no VAT added.
