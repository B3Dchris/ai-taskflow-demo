Position

Front headlock (you control the head)

Their head is below yours. Hips back and heavy, control one arm, then choke (guillotine, d'arce) or circle behind.

Moves from here

Submission

  • Guillotine (arm-in / high elbow)
  • D'Arce choke
  • Anaconda choke from front headlock
  • High-wrist guillotine
  • Front headlock chain: guillotine, d'arce, anaconda & go-behind

Back take

  • Go-behind from front headlock → Turtle (top)

Ways to get here

  • Snap-down to front headlock from Standing
  • Sprawl from Standing
  • Double-leg defense: downblock and sprawl from Standing
  • Counter-wrestling from seated guard: front headlock and guillotine from Seated guard
