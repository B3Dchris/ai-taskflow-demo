Position

Side control (top)

Crossface and far underhook, hips low, near knee blocking their hip. Kill their frames, then go to mount or attack the arms.

Moves from here

Control

  • Side control: pins & pressure
  • Side control to mount → Mount

Submission

  • Americana
  • Kimura from side control
  • North-south choke
  • Knee on belly and far-side armbar
  • Reverse triangle from top (side control or turtle)
  • North-south kimura to armbar

Back take

  • Kimura trap from side control to the back → Back control

Ways to get here

  • Double-leg takedown from Standing
  • Single leg (run the pipe) from Standing
  • Wrestle-up from seated guard from Seated guard
  • Knee cut (knee slice) pass from Passing
  • Body lock pass from Passing
  • Toreando (bullfighter) pass from Passing
  • Leg drag pass from Passing
  • Over-under pass from Passing
  • Underhook to dogfight to single leg from Half guard
  • Half guard pass: flatten & free the leg from Half guard top
  • Side control escape: underhook to knees from Side (bottom)
  • Headquarters position: knee slice and backstep options from Passing
  • Smash pass from half guard from Half guard top
  • Double-under (stack) pass from Passing
  • Backstep pass from half guard top from Half guard top
  • Body lock takedown with outside trip from Standing
  • Inside position passing: hands and knees first from Passing
