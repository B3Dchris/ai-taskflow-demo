Position

Seated / open guard (bottom)

Sit up, frames out, feet active. Keep your guard, then sweep, go for the legs, or wrestle up.

Moves from here

Entry

  • Shin-to-shin to single-leg X → Leg entangle
  • K-guard entry to the saddle (backside entanglement) → Leg entangle

Guard

  • Guard retention: frames & hip escape
  • De La Riva guard: hook and control
  • Guard retention against heavy pressure: frames and leg pummeling
  • Counter-wrestling from seated guard: front headlock and guillotine → Front headlock

Sweep

  • Wrestle-up from seated guard → Side control
  • Reverse De La Riva sweep and back exposure → Passing
  • Single-leg X sweep → Passing
  • 50/50: entry, sweep and heel exposure → Leg entangle

Escape

  • Technical stand-up → Standing

Defense

  • Knee cut defense: knee shield and underhook → Half guard
  • Body lock pass defense

Back take

  • Berimbolo from De La Riva to the back → Back control

Ways to get here

  • Sitting to guard safely from Standing
  • Side control escape: frame & re-guard from Side (bottom)
  • Turtle: defend & recover guard from Turtled
  • Knee on belly escape from Side (bottom)
  • North-south escape from Side (bottom)
  • Stopping the back take from turtle or a scramble from Turtled
