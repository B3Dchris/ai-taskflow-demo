Position

Under side control

Frame with bones, don't push with your arms. Bridge, shrimp, knee in to recover guard, or underhook up to your knees. Keep your near arm in.

Escape

  • Side control escape: frame & re-guard → Seated guard
  • Side control escape: underhook to knees → Side control
  • Knee on belly escape → Seated guard
  • North-south escape → Seated guard
  • Ghost escape from side control → Turtled

Defense

  • Kimura and americana defense
  • North-south choke defense

Submission

  • Buggy choke from under side control
