Position

Back control (you're behind)

The highest-value position. Seatbelt first, then hooks or a body triangle, then hunt the choke. Fight their hands before you force it.

Moves from here

Control

  • Back control: seatbelt & hooks
  • Back to mount → Mount

Submission

  • Rear-naked choke
  • Armbar from back control when they defend the choke
  • Arm-trap rear-naked choke
  • Rear-naked choke against a defended chin
  • Calf slicer from the truck
  • Banana split from the truck

Back take

  • Back retention: re-seating hooks when they slide out
  • Back attack hand-fight: two-on-one strip to choke or armbar

Ways to get here

  • Arm drag to the back from Standing
  • Arm drag from butterfly to the back from Butterfly
  • Mount to back take from Mount
  • Back take from turtle from Turtle (top)
  • Truck entry from turtle to back take from Turtle (top)
  • Berimbolo from De La Riva to the back from Seated guard
  • Kimura trap from side control to the back from Side control
  • Duck-under to the back from Standing
  • Power half and spiral ride from turtle from Turtle (top)
  • Arm drag to body lock to mat return from Standing
  • Russian tie (two-on-one) to the back or single leg from Standing
