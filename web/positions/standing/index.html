Position

Standing

Stance and hand fighting first. Snap down, shoot, or sit to guard when you choose to, not when they make you.

Moves from here

Takedown

  • Double-leg takedown → Side control
  • Single leg (run the pipe) → Side control
  • Snap-down to front headlock → Front headlock
  • Ankle pick takedown → Passing
  • High crotch takedown → Passing
  • Body lock takedown with outside trip → Side control
  • Duck-under to the back → Back control
  • Arm drag to body lock to mat return → Back control

Entry

  • Sitting to guard safely → Seated guard
  • Imanari roll to leg entanglement → Leg entangle

Escape

  • Sprawl → Front headlock

Defense

  • Single-leg defense: whizzer, hip pressure and limp leg
  • Double-leg defense: downblock and sprawl → Front headlock

Control

  • Stance, motion & hand fighting

Back take

  • Arm drag to the back → Back control

Ways to get here

  • Technical stand-up from Seated guard
  • Front headlock & guillotine defense from Headlocked
