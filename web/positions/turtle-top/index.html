Position

They're turtled (you're on top)

Seatbelt before hooks. Stay chest-to-back so they can't sit out, roll, or stand.

Moves from here

Control

  • Power half and spiral ride from turtle → Back control

Submission

  • Crucifix from turtle and the crucifix choke

Back take

  • Back take from turtle → Back control
  • Truck entry from turtle to back take → Back control

Ways to get here

  • Go-behind from front headlock from Front headlock
  • Wrestling switch from turtle from Turtled
