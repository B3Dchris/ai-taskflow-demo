Position

Leg entanglement (you're attacking)

Control the knee line, keep your own heels hidden, then finish the foot. Know your gym's rules on heel hooks.

Moves from here

Control

  • Ashi garami / single-leg X control
  • Saddle (inside sankaku) control: breaking the knee line

Submission

  • Straight ankle lock
  • Heel hook (know it, defend it)
  • Kneebar from outside position (ashi)
  • Inside heel hook from the saddle
  • Outside heel hook from outside ashi
  • Backside 50/50 heel hook
  • Toe hold from outside ashi

Ways to get here

  • Shin-to-shin to single-leg X from Seated guard
  • K-guard entry to the saddle (backside entanglement) from Seated guard
  • 50/50: entry, sweep and heel exposure from Seated guard
  • Counter leg lock: clear the knee line into your own attack from Legs attacked
  • Peterson roll (sit-out) from turtle to leg entanglement from Turtled
  • Imanari roll to leg entanglement from Standing
