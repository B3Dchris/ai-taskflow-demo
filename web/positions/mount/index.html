Position

Mount (top)

Stay low and heavy and widen your base on every bridge. Climb to high mount when they push. Attacks: armbar, arm triangle, back take when they turn.

Moves from here

Control

  • Mount: stay & climb

Submission

  • Armbar from mount
  • Arm triangle (head & arm)
  • Mounted triangle
  • Mount attack chain: arm triangle, armbar, back take
  • Mounted guillotine
  • No-gi Ezekiel choke from mount

Back take

  • Mount to back take → Back control

Ways to get here

  • Butterfly (hook) sweep from Butterfly
  • Hip bump sweep from Closed guard
  • Side control to mount from Side control
  • Back to mount from Back control
  • Pendulum (flower) sweep from closed guard from Closed guard
  • Leg weave pass to mount from Passing
