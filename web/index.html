02The chains

For the days between classes

You learn an armbar on Tuesday and it's gone by Thursday. Or you know five techniques but not which one to use, or what to do when the first one fails. JitsFlow lays out every position with its options and the usual reactions, so you can go over it at home instead of working it out mid-roll.

What to do when they defend

Every attacking position has a tree of what to do next. Start with the setup; each branch is how they react and what you switch to. This is the closed guard page, as the app has it.

  1. pull them down so they can't posture Closed guard: break posture & control
    1. they sit up tall to escape Hip bump sweep
      1. they post a hand on the mat to stop the sweep Kimura from closed guard
      2. they lean forward to flatten the sweep Guillotine from guard
    2. they put a hand on your chest or hip Armbar from closed guard
      1. they pull the elbow out and stack you Triangle choke
      2. they yank the arm back and sit up Hip bump sweep
  2. one arm is in, one is out Triangle choke
    1. they pull the trapped arm back toward their hip Armbar from closed guard
    2. they posture and turn the trapped arm away Omoplata from closed guard
  3. they reach an arm deep under you Shoulder clamp (overhook guard) to triangle or back
    1. they try to posture out of the clamp Triangle choke
  4. they stay low and heavy, gripping your hips Pendulum (flower) sweep from closed guard

The same chains from the bottom

Flip any position and the same tree shows what they'll go for, how to stop each attack, and what they'll switch to when you do. 38 escapes and submission defenses.

06Questions

Before you start

Does it replace my coach?

No. JitsFlow is for study between classes. Coaches teach details differently, so moves where that matters are flagged with "Check with your coach", and each belt has a coach review list to go through together.

I run a gym. What does JitsFlow do for gyms?

Timetable, bookings, waitlists and check-in, memberships and fees with reminders, attendance insights, competitions and WhatsApp invites. Members pay by EFT with their own reference today; card, Apple Pay and Google Pay are coming soon. See JitsFlow for coaches, or start your gym. Existing coaches tap Sign in at the top of this page.

What does it cost?

Studying is free, with no account needed. A gym is free for its first 3 months, then R99 a month, and free for its students.

Is it only for no-gi?

Every technique and video is chosen for no-gi, and most of it carries straight over to the gi. The gym tools work for any gym.

Can I read the techniques without the app?

Yes. Every move has its own page in the technique library, with steps, details, common mistakes, a drill and a video. There's also a glossary of BJJ terms and the belt curriculum.

Where's my data?

On your phone, unless you create a free account. Then your progress is saved to your account so it's on any device, and you can delete everything in Settings. See the privacy notice.

Does it work at the gym with no signal?

Yes. Once opened, JitsFlow works offline. Videos need a connection.

How do I put it on my home screen?

iPhone: open jitsflow.com in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome and tap Install app. It opens full screen, like any app.

Who made the videos?

Instructors on YouTube. JitsFlow links each move to a clear instructional video and plays it from YouTube; the videos belong to their creators.

Is it safe to learn leg locks from an app?

Learn the basics here, but always learn finishes, especially heel hooks, from a coach in person. JitsFlow deliberately leaves out heel hook finishing directions. Tap early in training.
