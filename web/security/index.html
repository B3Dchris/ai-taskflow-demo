Secure payments

Secure payments

Card payments go through Payfast (a gym's JitsFlow plan) and Paystack (members paying their gym), both PCI DSS compliant payment processors. Your card details go to them only, never to JitsFlow.

How it works

  • When you pay or add a card, JitsFlow sends you to Payfast's or Paystack's secure checkout, where you enter your card or choose another payment method. Both are PCI DSS compliant, the card industry's security standard.
  • JitsFlow never sees, receives or stores your full card number or security code (CVV).
  • For monthly billing or a saved card, Payfast or Paystack gives us a token (Paystack calls it an authorization): a reference that lets us ask them to charge that card. A token isn't your card number and is useless outside the processor. Saved-card tokens are encrypted when we store them.
  • We only mark a payment as paid when the processor's notification about it has a valid signature and the processor confirms it directly, for the amount and account the checkout asked for.
  • Membership money is paid by Paystack straight into the gym's bank account. JitsFlow never holds it. A gym's bank and ID details are encrypted when we store them, and shown only by their last 4 digits.
  • The whole site uses HTTPS (encrypted connections), and browsers are told to always use it (HSTS).

Ways to pay

Gym plans are paid to JitsFlow by card (Visa and Mastercard) or another method Payfast offers at its checkout. Which ones you see depends on your device. All amounts are in South African rand (ZAR).

Members pay their gym by EFT with their own reference today. Card, Apple Pay and Capitec Pay are coming soon: card payments will be processed by Paystack and paid by Paystack directly into the gym's bank account, so JitsFlow never holds membership money.
