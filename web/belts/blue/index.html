White to Blue belt

Learn to survive, escape and understand the positions. A blue belt can't easily be held down or submitted by beginners, knows what they want in every position, and has a few moves that work in live rolls.

Typically 1.5–3 years of steady training; often closer to 3 at 2 sessions a week.

What coaches look for

  • Escape mount, side control and the back against a same-size white belt going hard
  • Survive rounds with higher belts without getting tapped every time
  • Know every position on the flow map and your goal in each
  • Pass the guard one reliable way and hold side control
  • Sweep or come up from half guard against resistance
  • Finish 2–3 submissions in live rolls
  • Defend leg locks safely: hide the heel, clear the knee line, tap early
  • Train consistently and be a safe, controlled partner

How to get there faster

  • Defense first: survival is what coaches look at most
  • Pick one game plan and run it every roll for a month
  • Log every session so your weak spots show up
  • Ask your coach directly what they want to see from you

The 74 techniques

Takedown

  • Double-leg takedown
  • Single leg (run the pipe)
  • Snap-down to front headlock

Entry

  • Sitting to guard safely
  • Shin-to-shin to single-leg X

Guard

  • Guard retention: frames & hip escape
  • Half guard bottom: knee shield & underhook
  • Recover full guard from half

Sweep

  • Wrestle-up from seated guard
  • Butterfly (hook) sweep
  • Hip bump sweep
  • Underhook to dogfight to single leg

Pass

  • Posture up & open closed guard
  • Knee cut (knee slice) pass
  • Body lock pass
  • Toreando (bullfighter) pass
  • Leg drag pass
  • Over-under pass
  • Half guard pass: flatten & free the leg

Escape

  • Sprawl
  • Technical stand-up
  • Side control escape: frame & re-guard
  • Side control escape: underhook to knees
  • Trap & roll (upa)
  • Elbow-knee escape (shrimp)
  • Back escape
  • Turtle: defend & recover guard
  • Front headlock & guillotine defense
  • Leg lock defense
  • Knee on belly escape
  • North-south escape
  • Stopping the back take from turtle or a scramble
  • Mount defense: protect your neck and arms

Defense

  • Armbar defense: closed guard and mount
  • Triangle defense
  • Kimura and americana defense
  • Arm triangle defense
  • Rear-naked choke defense
  • Guillotine defense: in their guard and standing
  • D'Arce and anaconda defense
  • Straight ankle lock defense
  • North-south choke defense
  • Single-leg defense: whizzer, hip pressure and limp leg
  • Double-leg defense: downblock and sprawl
  • Knee cut defense: knee shield and underhook
  • Body lock pass defense

Control

  • Stance, motion & hand fighting
  • Closed guard: break posture & control
  • Half guard top: crossface & flatten
  • Side control: pins & pressure
  • Side control to mount
  • Mount: stay & climb
  • Back control: seatbelt & hooks
  • Back to mount
  • Ashi garami / single-leg X control

Submission

  • Guillotine from guard
  • Triangle choke
  • Armbar from closed guard
  • Americana
  • Kimura from side control
  • North-south choke
  • Armbar from mount
  • Arm triangle (head & arm)
  • Rear-naked choke
  • Guillotine (arm-in / high elbow)
  • D'Arce choke
  • Straight ankle lock
  • Heel hook (know it, defend it)

Back take

  • Arm drag to the back
  • Arm drag from butterfly to the back
  • Mount to back take
  • Back take from turtle
  • Go-behind from front headlock
