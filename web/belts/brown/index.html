Purple to Brown belt

Chains, counters and pressure. A brown belt sees attacks two moves ahead, counters the counters, and controls stronger people with position and timing. Where your rules allow it, the leg-lock game is complete.

Typically 1.5–2 years at purple. IBJJF minimum: 1.5 years at purple, age 18+.

What coaches look for

  • You link attacks across positions without resetting
  • You counter the common counters to your own moves
  • You have a complete leg-lock system: entries, control, finishes and counters
  • You pressure pass and pin higher-level partners
  • Purple belts rarely submit you
  • You coach during rolls and fix other people's details

How to get there faster

  • Train specific problems, not just open rolls
  • Drill the counters to your favorite moves
  • Teach regularly; it shows what you don't understand yet
  • Study high-level competition footage of your positions

The 23 techniques

Takedown

  • Body lock takedown with outside trip
  • Duck-under to the back

Entry

  • K-guard entry to the saddle (backside entanglement)

Guard

  • Shoulder clamp (overhook guard) to triangle or back

Sweep

  • 50/50: entry, sweep and heel exposure

Pass

  • Backstep pass from half guard top
  • Leg weave pass to mount

Escape

  • Counter leg lock: clear the knee line into your own attack
  • Leg lock defense from the saddle: hand fight & escape
  • Peterson roll (sit-out) from turtle to leg entanglement

Control

  • Saddle (inside sankaku) control: breaking the knee line

Submission

  • Inside heel hook from the saddle
  • Outside heel hook from outside ashi
  • Mount attack chain: arm triangle, armbar, back take
  • Front headlock chain: guillotine, d'arce, anaconda & go-behind
  • Japanese necktie from front headlock
  • Peruvian necktie from front headlock
  • Gogoplata from closed guard
  • Calf slicer from the truck
  • Toe hold from outside ashi

Back take

  • Truck entry from turtle to back take
  • Berimbolo from De La Riva to the back
  • Kimura trap from side control to the back
