Curriculum

Blue to Purple belt

Build systems. A purple belt has a guard that threatens sweeps and submissions off the same grips, passes in chains, attacks the back as a system, and starts to set the pace in rolls. Many purple belts start helping teach the basics.

Typically about 2 years at blue belt. IBJJF minimum: 2 years at blue.

What coaches look for

  • Your guard is hard to pass and attacks with sweeps and submissions together
  • You pass with at least two linked options
  • You take the back from three or more positions and finish from it
  • You enter and finish a straight ankle lock and defend heel hooks calmly
  • You hold dominant positions against blue belts going hard
  • You can explain and teach the white-belt fundamentals

How to get there faster

  • Specialize: build one guard deeply (butterfly/X/SLX, or half guard)
  • Do positional rounds from your worst positions every week
  • Compete a few times if you can; it shows your gaps quickly
  • Film some of your rolls and watch them back

The 31 techniques

Takedown

  • Ankle pick takedown
  • High crotch takedown

Guard

  • De La Riva guard: hook and control

Sweep

  • Reverse De La Riva sweep and back exposure
  • X-guard entry and technical stand-up sweep
  • Single-leg X sweep
  • Pendulum (flower) sweep from closed guard
  • Kimura trap from half guard bottom: sweep or back take
  • Waiter sweep from deep half guard
  • Lockdown half guard and old-school sweep

Pass

  • Headquarters position: knee slice and backstep options
  • Smash pass from half guard
  • Double-under (stack) pass

Escape

  • Crucifix escape
  • Body triangle escape from the back
  • Kipping (heel-drag) mount escape
  • Ghost escape from side control

Defense

  • Omoplata defense: roll forward and recover
  • Kneebar defense
  • Mounted triangle defense
  • Kimura trap defense from half guard top

Submission

  • Omoplata from closed guard
  • Knee on belly and far-side armbar
  • Mounted triangle
  • Armbar from back control when they defend the choke
  • Arm-trap rear-naked choke
  • Crucifix from turtle and the crucifix choke
  • Anaconda choke from front headlock
  • High-wrist guillotine
  • Kneebar from outside position (ashi)

Back take

  • Back retention: re-seating hooks when they slide out
