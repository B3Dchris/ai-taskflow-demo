Knee tap takedown

A collar tie takedown where you block their knee and pull their head across. Use it when they stand tall with their weight on their lead leg.

Standing → Passing

Step by step

  1. From a collar tie, pull their head down and toward the side of the knee you will tap.
  2. Step your lead foot in close to their lead foot.
  3. Drop your level by bending your knees. Put your free hand on the outside of their near knee.
  4. Keep their knee blocked with your hand while you pull the collar tie down and across their body. They fall toward the blocked knee.
  5. Follow them down, keep your weight on them, and start passing.

Details that make it work

  • The collar tie moves their head over the blocked knee. Head and knee work together.
  • Block the knee, don't grab it. The hand is a wall they trip over.

Common mistakes

  • Reaching for the knee from too far away, with your back bent.
  • Pulling the head straight down instead of down and across.

Drill it

Partner in stance at 50 percent. 10 knee taps each side, following them down to a pass.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Knee cut (knee slice) pass Pass · Passing
  • Toreando (bullfighter) pass Pass · Passing
  • Side control: pins & pressure Control · Side control
