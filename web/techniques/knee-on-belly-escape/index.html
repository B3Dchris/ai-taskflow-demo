Knee on belly escape

How to get out from under knee on belly: frame the knee, shrimp away and re-guard. Use it as soon as they pop up, before they settle.

Side (bottom) → Seated guard

Step by step

  1. Frame the moment they pop up: both hands on their knee and shin. Don't let them settle their weight.
  2. Turn onto your side toward them. Flat on your back is where knee on belly hurts.
  3. Push their knee down toward your hips while you shrimp your hips away from it.
  4. As their knee slides off your belly, bring your near knee into the space between you.
  5. Recover guard with your frames. If you can't get the knee in, turn to your knees.

Details that make it work

  • Escape early. Each second they settle, the escape gets harder.
  • Move your hips, not just your hands. The push makes space; the shrimp uses it.

Common mistakes

  • Pushing their chest or face with straight arms (armbar).
  • Pushing the knee without moving your hips.

Drill it

Partner holds knee on belly: 10 escapes each side, then 2 × 1 min positional.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Guard retention: frames & hip escape Guard · Seated guard
  • Side control escape: frame & re-guard Escape · Side (bottom)
