Knee cut (knee slice) pass

You slide your knee across their thigh to pass to side control. It's the most common pass in no-gi and works against both half guard and open guard.

Passing → Side control

Step by step

  1. Control their near knee: pin it to the mat or hold it.
  2. Get an underhook on their far arm, or crossface (your shoulder into their jaw).
  3. Slide your knee across their thigh to the mat on the far side.
  4. Keep your back leg wide for base and your chest heavy on theirs.
  5. Pull your trailing leg free and settle in side control.

Details that make it work

  • Underhook first. Without it they turn in and recover half guard.
  • Keep your head low, on the far side of theirs.

Common mistakes

  • Slicing while sitting upright.
  • Leaving your foot trapped between their legs.

Drill it

10 passes each side, each ending in a 3-second side control hold.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Side control: pins & pressure Control · Side control
  • Side control to mount Control · Side control

Set it up from

  • Double-leg takedown
  • Posture up & open closed guard
  • Half guard top: crossface & flatten
  • Leg lock defense
  • Triangle defense
  • Straight ankle lock defense
  • Omoplata defense: roll forward and recover
  • Reverse De La Riva sweep and back exposure
  • X-guard entry and technical stand-up sweep
  • Single-leg X sweep

How to defend it

Knee cut defense: knee shield and underhook: How to stop the knee cut: get on your side, knee shield, and win the underhook. Use it as soon as they start slicing across your thigh.
