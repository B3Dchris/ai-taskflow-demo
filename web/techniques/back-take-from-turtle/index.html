Back take from turtle

From on top of a turtled opponent, take a seatbelt, put a hook in, and roll them to take the back.

Turtle (top) → Back control

Step by step

  1. From behind or beside their turtle, get a seatbelt: one arm over the shoulder, one under the armpit.
  2. Chest tight on their back.
  3. Put in the hook on the side of your under arm, near their hip.
  4. Roll to your side, bringing them on top of you, and put in the second hook.

Details that make it work

  • Seatbelt first, then hooks.
  • Roll toward your under-arm side.

Common mistakes

  • Putting hooks in before you have the seatbelt.
  • Rolling to the wrong side.

Drill it

10 reps.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Back control: seatbelt & hooks Control · Back control
  • Rear-naked choke Submission · Back control

Set it up from

  • Arm drag to the back
  • Leg drag pass
  • Go-behind from front headlock
  • Duck-under to the back
  • Arm drag to body lock to mat return
  • Wrestling switch from turtle
