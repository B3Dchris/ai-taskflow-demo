Body lock takedown with outside trip

You lock your hands around their back, pull their hips in and trip them backward over your leg. Use it when you get double underhooks or a body lock standing.

Standing → Side control

Step by step

  1. Get to a body lock: both arms under their arms, hands locked low on their back, head tight to their chest.
  2. Pull their hips in tight to yours and step to an angle on one side.
  3. Step your leg behind their near leg, from the outside, your calf against the back of their heel.
  4. Drive your chest into them diagonally back over the trapped leg. They fall backward.
  5. Fall with them, keep the body lock and land chest to chest. Pass to side control.

Details that make it work

  • Hips in first. A body lock with space between you won't trip anyone.
  • Your chest drives them back, your leg only blocks.

Common mistakes

  • Reaching the leg before their weight is on it.
  • Lifting and dropping them. Take them down; never slam.

Drill it

10 takedowns each side onto a crash pad or soft mat, partner breakfalls.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Body lock pass Pass · Passing
  • Side control: pins & pressure Control · Side control
