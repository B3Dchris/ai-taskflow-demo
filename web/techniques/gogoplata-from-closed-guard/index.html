Gogoplata from closed guard

A choke from closed guard with your shin under their chin and their head pulled down. Use it when you have their posture broken and your hips are flexible enough to bring the shin in front.

Closed guard → Submission

Step by step

  1. From closed guard, break their posture and overhook one arm tight to your chest so their head stays low.
  2. Climb your legs high on their back. On the overhook side, bring your knee up so your leg sits over their shoulder with their arm trapped under it.
  3. Hold your ankle with your free hand and pull your foot past the front of their face, so your shin slides under their chin and your foot ends past their far ear.
  4. Cup the back of their head with both hands, or hold their head with one hand and your toes with the other.
  5. Keep their trapped arm inside your leg so they cannot push your shin away.
  6. Finish slowly. Pull their head down onto your shin and curl your hips up a little at a time. Release on the tap.

Details that make it work

  • Your shin must be under the chin, not on the jaw or face.
  • This needs open hips. Warm up your hips and knees first and stop if your own knee hurts.

Common mistakes

  • Trying it with their arm outside your leg, so they push your shin straight off.
  • Yanking the head down fast, which hurts the throat before they can tap.

Drill it

From closed guard with a cooperative partner. 3 rounds of 1 minute: break posture, overhook, place the shin, hold 3 seconds with no squeeze, reset.
