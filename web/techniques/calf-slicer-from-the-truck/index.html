Calf slicer from the truck

A compression lock on the knee from the truck, folding their calf around your shin. Use it when you hold the truck and they block the back take, only where calf slicers are allowed.

Back control → Submission

Step by step

  1. Get to the truck: sit behind and to the side of them with your legs locked around their near leg, your leg threaded behind their knee.
  2. Make sure your shin is deep in the back of their trapped knee. Keep the lock on their leg tight.
  3. Reach down and hug their trapped ankle and foot to your chest with both arms.
  4. Keep their upper body turned away from you with your chest and shoulder so they cannot sit up into you.
  5. Finish slowly. Pull their foot toward your chest while you extend your hips and locked legs away, folding their calf around your shin a little at a time. Release on the tap.

Details that make it work

  • Your shin is the blade. It must sit deep in the knee pit or there is no pressure.
  • The finish is a slow fold. Calf slicers damage the knee before they hurt much.

Common mistakes

  • Grabbing the foot before your shin is deep in the knee.
  • Jerking the foot toward you instead of folding the leg slowly.

Drill it

From the truck with a partner who knows the move. 6 entries each side to the foot grip with no pressure. Finish only slowly, with a partner you trust.
