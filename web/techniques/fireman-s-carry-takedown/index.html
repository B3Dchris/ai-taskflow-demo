Fireman's carry takedown

A wrestling takedown where you drop under their arm and roll them across your shoulders. Use it when they reach with an arm or lean heavy on your collar tie.

Standing → Side control

Step by step

  1. From a collar tie or two-on-one, control their arm just above the elbow.
  2. Pull the arm down and across so their weight goes onto their near leg.
  3. Drop step between their feet onto your knees, with their arm over your shoulder and your head on the outside of their body.
  4. Reach your free arm between their legs and hook the near leg.
  5. Keep pulling the arm, turn your head away, and roll them across your shoulders. They land on their back and side.
  6. Follow them and settle chest to chest in side control.

Details that make it work

  • Keep hold of their arm all the way down. That is what puts them on their back and not their head.
  • Get your hips under their hips. If you are too far away, you just pull them down on top of you.

Common mistakes

  • Lifting them high and dropping them.
  • Letting go of the arm, so they post and land on top.

Drill it

On crash pads first. 5 slow reps each side with a partner who knows how to fall, then 5 at normal speed each side.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Side control: pins & pressure Control · Side control
  • Side control to mount Control · Side control
  • Knee on belly and far-side armbar Submission · Side control
