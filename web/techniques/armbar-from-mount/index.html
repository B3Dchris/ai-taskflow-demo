Armbar from mount

From high mount, isolate one arm, step to S-mount, and sit back into the armbar. It's the classic finish when they push on you.

Mount → Submission

Step by step

  1. From high mount, push one arm across their body; hug it to your chest.
  2. Step into S-mount: one knee up beside their head, the other foot tucked under their side.
  3. Swing your leg over their face and sit back, knees squeezed and their arm tight to you.
  4. Thumb up, hips up. Slow.

Details that make it work

  • Stay heavy in S-mount. Sitting back too early lets them escape.
  • Squeeze your knees together the whole time.

Common mistakes

  • Sitting back with space between your butt and their shoulder.
  • Letting go of the arm when they roll.

Drill it

5 reps each side.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Arm triangle (head & arm) Submission · Mount

Set it up from

  • Side control to mount
  • Mount: stay & climb
  • Back to mount
  • Knee on belly and far-side armbar
  • Mounted triangle
  • Mount attack chain: arm triangle, armbar, back take
  • Reverse triangle from top (side control or turtle)
  • North-south kimura to armbar
