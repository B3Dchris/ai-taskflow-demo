Heel hook (know it, defend it)

A knee-twisting attack that's powerful and dangerous. Know how it works so you can defend it, and only train it where your coach allows.

Leg entangle → Submission

Step by step

  1. Only drill it when your coach and class allow it.
  2. Mechanics: their heel is trapped in the crook of your arm; turning the heel twists the knee.
  3. Finish slowly. Knee damage can happen before pain. Partners tap as soon as the grip locks.

Details that make it work

  • If your gym doesn't drill it yet, learn the defense first.
  • Never spin out of a heel hook that's already locked.

Common mistakes

  • Cranking fast.
  • A partner who won't tap.

Drill it

Defense only until coached: hide the heel, clear the knee line.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Leg lock defense Escape · Legs attacked

Set it up from

  • Ashi garami / single-leg X control
  • Straight ankle lock
  • Imanari roll to leg entanglement
  • Backside 50/50 heel hook

How to defend it

Leg lock defense: How to escape a leg entanglement: hide the heel, pull your knee past their hips, and get out. Tap early in training.
