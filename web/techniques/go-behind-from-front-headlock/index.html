Go-behind from front headlock

From the front headlock, circle around to their back while keeping your weight on their head. If the choke isn't there, take the back.

Front headlock → Turtle (top)

Step by step

  1. Control their head and one arm.
  2. Step around to the side of the arm you're controlling, chest heavy on their back.
  3. Circle until you're behind them; take a seatbelt or body lock.
  4. Put in hooks, or flatten them out.

Details that make it work

  • Keep pressure on their head while you circle.
  • If they try to come up, snap them back down.

Common mistakes

  • Letting their head come up.

Drill it

10 reps each side.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Back take from turtle Back take · Turtle (top)

Set it up from

  • Snap-down to front headlock
  • Sprawl
  • D'Arce choke
  • Double-leg defense: downblock and sprawl
  • Anaconda choke from front headlock
  • High-wrist guillotine
  • Front headlock chain: guillotine, d'arce, anaconda & go-behind
  • Counter-wrestling from seated guard: front headlock and guillotine
