North-south choke defense

Defense against the north-south choke: hands inside and get off your back. Use it whenever they walk around to north-south.

Side (bottom) → Side (bottom)

Step by step

  1. Prevent it: when they walk to north-south, keep your elbows tight and your hands at your neck. Don't reach around their back.
  2. If their arm slides under your neck, get a hand or forearm inside, between their arm and your throat.
  3. Bridge and walk your feet to turn your body, so you're on your side and out of line with them.
  4. Keep turning until you're at their side, then frame and escape side control.
  5. Tap when their arm is under your neck and they sink their weight with your hands still outside.

Details that make it work

  • Hands inside before they sink their hips.
  • Get off your back. Flat and square under them is where the choke works.

Common mistakes

  • Pushing their head with a straight arm.
  • Holding still and hoping the squeeze isn't tight.

Drill it

Partner in north-south hunts the choke at 50%: 3 × 1 min positional.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Side control escape: frame & re-guard Escape · Side (bottom)
  • North-south escape Escape · Side (bottom)
