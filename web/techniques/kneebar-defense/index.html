Kneebar defense

How to defend a kneebar: keep the leg bent, fight the grip and pull your knee away from their hips. Use it when they hug your leg from a leg entanglement or while you pass, and tap early.

Legs attacked → Passing

Step by step

  1. Prevent it: keep your knee bent and don't leave a straight leg between theirs when you pass or when they enter a leg entanglement.
  2. If they hug your leg, bend it hard and bring your heel toward your butt. They can't lock a bent leg.
  3. Sit up or turn toward them and fight their grip on your foot.
  4. Pull your knee back toward you so it isn't stretched across their hips.
  5. Free the leg, then stand or pass.
  6. Tap when they arch their hips and you feel pressure behind the knee. Knee ligaments can tear before it hurts much. Don't try to spin or roll out of a locked kneebar.

Details that make it work

  • A bent leg is a safe leg.
  • Their hips are the fulcrum. Get your knee away from them.

Common mistakes

  • Pushing their head with your free foot while the trapped leg goes straight.
  • Waiting for pain before tapping.

Drill it

Partner attacks the kneebar slowly from the top of a leg entanglement: 5 escapes each leg. Tap early in every rep.
