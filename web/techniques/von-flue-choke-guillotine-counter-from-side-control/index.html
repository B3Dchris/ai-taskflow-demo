Von Flue choke: guillotine counter from side control

A counter to the guillotine where you pass to the far side and choke them with your shoulder and their own arm. Use it when they keep holding a guillotine after you start to pass.

Side control → Submission

Step by step

  1. When they hold an arm-out guillotine from guard, put both hands on their choking wrist and pull it down to free your throat.
  2. Pass to side control on the side away from their choking arm, the opposite side from where your head is trapped. Your legs end on one side of their body and your head on the other.
  3. Slide your leading arm under their head. Bring your trailing arm over the wrist and forearm of their choking arm to keep it wrapped around your neck. Clasp your hands palm to palm.
  4. Squeeze your far shoulder up to your ear so they cannot drop the arm and unwrap.
  5. Sprawl your legs back and come up on your toes.
  6. Finish slowly. Drive your weight down through your near shoulder toward their head. Your shoulder closes one side of the neck and their own arm closes the other. Release on the tap.

Details that make it work

  • Go to the side away from their choking arm. On the same side the guillotine gets tighter.
  • If they let go of the neck, stop the choke and attack the free arm with a kimura or americana.

Common mistakes

  • Moving to side control with your weight off them, so they roll you while the guillotine is still on.
  • Squeezing hard and fast. This choke works quickly and can knock someone out.

Drill it

Partner locks a loose arm-out guillotine from guard. 5 passes each side to the locked hands, then 3 slow finishes each side.
