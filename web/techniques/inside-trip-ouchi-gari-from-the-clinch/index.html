Inside trip (ouchi gari) from the clinch

A clinch takedown that hooks one leg from the inside and drives them backward. Use it when they lean into you or stand with their feet wide.

Standing → Passing

Step by step

  1. From an over-under clinch or a collar tie and elbow, pull them close so your chest is on theirs.
  2. Step in deep between their feet.
  3. Hook one of their legs from the inside with your leg, the back of your calf against the back of their calf or knee.
  4. Reap that leg out to the side and back while you drive your chest forward into them.
  5. They fall onto their back. Stay chest to chest, land on top, and start passing.

Details that make it work

  • Chest pressure and the reap happen at the same time.
  • Hook low on the leg, at the calf or knee, not the thigh.

Common mistakes

  • Reaching for the leg from a distance, so you stand on one leg in front of them.
  • Driving forward with your head down, so they sprawl.

Drill it

Partner in clinch at 50 percent. 10 inside trips each side, landing in a passing position.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Knee cut (knee slice) pass Pass · Passing
  • Body lock pass Pass · Passing
  • Half guard pass: flatten & free the leg Pass · Half guard top
