Granby roll from turtle to guard

A shoulder roll from turtle that brings your legs back in front of them. Use it when they are about to take your back and you need to recover guard fast.

Turtled → Seated guard

Step by step

  1. From turtle with them on your back or side, tuck your chin and look under your body toward your far hip.
  2. Post on your feet and drive one shoulder to the mat under you.
  3. Roll across your shoulders, not your neck, pulling your knees toward your chest as you go.
  4. Keep turning until your hips face them and your legs come between you.
  5. Put your feet on their hips or knees and frame with your hands. Recover your guard.

Details that make it work

  • Roll on your shoulders. Tuck your chin and turn your head away so your neck stays out of it.
  • Keep your knees tight to your chest through the roll so your legs come back fast.

Common mistakes

  • Rolling straight over your head.
  • Stopping halfway on your back with your legs still behind you.

Drill it

Solo first: 10 Granby rolls each side on the mat. Then partner rides your turtle at 50 percent, 5 rolls each side to guard.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Guard retention: frames & hip escape Guard · Seated guard
  • Wrestle-up from seated guard Sweep · Seated guard
  • Shin-to-shin to single-leg X Entry · Seated guard
