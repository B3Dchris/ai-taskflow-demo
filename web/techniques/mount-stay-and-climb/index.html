Mount: stay & climb

How to stay in mount when they bridge and push: low base, grapevines, and climbing high. If you can't hold mount, you can't attack from it.

Mount → Mount

Step by step

  1. Low mount: knees tight to their hips, chest low, hands posted wide.
  2. Grapevine their legs (hook under them) when they bridge.
  3. When they push you, climb: walk your knees up toward their armpits (high mount).
  4. Keep your weight on them, not on your knees.
  5. Answer every bridge by widening your base.

Details that make it work

  • High mount stops elbow-knee escapes because they can't get their knees up.
  • Sit on them, don't kneel over them.

Common mistakes

  • Sitting upright with your hands off the mat.
  • Letting them trap an arm and bridge.

Drill it

Mount retention: partner tries upa and elbow-knee. 3 × 1 min.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Armbar from mount Submission · Mount
  • Arm triangle (head & arm) Submission · Mount
  • Mount to back take Back take · Mount

Set it up from

  • Butterfly (hook) sweep
  • Hip bump sweep
  • Body lock pass
  • Side control to mount
  • Arm triangle (head & arm)
  • Back to mount
  • Pendulum (flower) sweep from closed guard
  • Leg weave pass to mount
  • Mounted guillotine
  • No-gi Ezekiel choke from mount
