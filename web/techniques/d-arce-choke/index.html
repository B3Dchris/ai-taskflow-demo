D'Arce choke

A choke from front headlock or half guard top: your arm threads under their arm and neck, trapping both. Use it when they shoot or dig for an underhook.

Front headlock → Submission

Step by step

  1. From the front headlock, thread your arm under their near arm and under their neck, out the other side.
  2. Grip your other biceps; put that hand behind their head or on their back.
  3. Squeeze your elbows together and walk your hips out to tighten.
  4. Roll them, or drop to your side, to finish if needed.

Details that make it work

  • Get your armpit deep against their shoulder.
  • A common counter when they shoot, or when they dig an underhook from half guard.
  • From turtle: get beside their head. When their near arm posts or reaches, thread under it and around the neck, then drop to your side.

Common mistakes

  • A shallow grip.
  • Not adding your bodyweight.

Drill it

5 reps each side.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Go-behind from front headlock Back take · Front headlock

Set it up from

  • Snap-down to front headlock
  • Sprawl
  • Guillotine (arm-in / high elbow)
  • D'Arce and anaconda defense
  • Anaconda choke from front headlock
  • Front headlock chain: guillotine, d'arce, anaconda & go-behind
  • Counter-wrestling from seated guard: front headlock and guillotine
  • Japanese necktie from front headlock
