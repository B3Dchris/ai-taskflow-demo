Stance, motion & hand fighting

The base of everything on the feet: how you stand, move and fight for grips. Get this right and they can't reach your legs, while your own attacks open up.

Standing → Standing

Step by step

  1. Staggered stance: feet about shoulder-width, lead foot slightly forward, knees bent, hips under your shoulders.
  2. Head up, eyes on their chest and hips, elbows tight to your ribs so nothing gets underhooked.
  3. Move in short steps and never cross your feet. Circle away from their strong hand.
  4. Win the hands first: clear their collar tie, take wrist control or a two-on-one before you attack.
  5. Change levels by bending your knees, not your back, every time you go for the legs.

Details that make it work

  • Your posture is your defense. If your head drops below your hips, you're giving them a snap-down.
  • Hand fighting is about being first: touch, clear, grip, attack, all within about a second.

Common mistakes

  • Standing tall and square, which makes you easy to shoot on.
  • Reaching for legs from too far away instead of closing distance with a tie-up first.

Drill it

Shadow wrestling, 3 × 1 min: stance, motion, level changes, penetration steps.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Snap-down to front headlock Takedown · Standing
  • Double-leg takedown Takedown · Standing
  • Single leg (run the pipe) Takedown · Standing
  • Arm drag to the back Back take · Standing

Set it up from

  • Technical stand-up
  • Front headlock & guillotine defense
  • Single-leg defense: whizzer, hip pressure and limp leg
