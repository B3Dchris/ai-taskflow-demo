Blue belt · Takedown · Key

Single leg (run the pipe)

You take one leg and finish by turning the corner or tripping the other leg. It's safer than a double when they stand in a staggered stance with one leg forward.

Standing → Side control

Step by step

  1. From a collar tie or arm drag, step in and drop your level to their lead leg.
  2. Grab behind the knee and lift the leg to your hip, head tight on the inside against their chest.
  3. Pinch the leg between your thighs or keep it high on your hip. Head up.
  4. Finish by running the pipe (turn the corner, drive your shoulder down through their thigh while stepping back), or trip the standing leg.
  5. Follow them to the mat and settle into side control.

Details that make it work

  • Head on the inside of the leg is safer from guillotines.
  • Turning the corner is done with your feet, not by pulling with your arms.

Common mistakes

  • Hugging the leg low with your head down at their knee.
  • Letting them whizzer and flatten you. Keep your hips under you.

Drill it

Partner gives a leg: 10 finishes each side (run the pipe, trip, lift).

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Side control: pins & pressure Control · Side control

Set it up from

  • Stance, motion & hand fighting
  • Wrestle-up from seated guard
  • Underhook to dogfight to single leg
  • Side control escape: underhook to knees
  • High crotch takedown
  • Ghost escape from side control
  • Stand-up from turtle
  • Russian tie (two-on-one) to the back or single leg

How to defend it

Single-leg defense: whizzer, hip pressure and limp leg: How to defend a single leg: whizzer, drive your hip in, then limp leg out. Use it whenever they grab one of your legs standing.
