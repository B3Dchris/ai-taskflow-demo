Side control escape: underhook to knees

From under side control you get an underhook and turn onto your knees, then wrestle up. Use it when they sit high on your chest or are moving between pins.

Side (bottom) → Side control

Step by step

  1. Frame with your near arm to make space at their hip.
  2. Bridge and dig your near arm under their armpit for a deep underhook.
  3. Shrimp away and turn in toward them, coming up onto your knees.
  4. Keep the underhook, drive in, and switch to a single leg or come up behind them.

Details that make it work

  • Go as they transition, when their weight shifts.
  • Turn to your knees in one motion.

Common mistakes

  • Turning away from them, which gives up the back.
  • A shallow underhook at their elbow.

Drill it

Partner in side control: 10 reps each side.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Underhook to dogfight to single leg Sweep · Half guard
  • Single leg (run the pipe) Takedown · Standing

Set it up from

  • Kimura and americana defense
  • North-south escape
  • Buggy choke from under side control
