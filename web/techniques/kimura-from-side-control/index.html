Kimura from side control

The same figure-four shoulder lock as from guard, but from side control. Use it when their near arm comes off their body to frame or push.

Side control → Submission

Step by step

  1. When their near arm comes away from their body, grab the wrist.
  2. Thread your other arm under their elbow and grab your own wrist (figure-four).
  3. Step over their head toward north-south, or stay chest-heavy.
  4. Pull their arm off their body and rotate the hand toward their head. Slow.

Details that make it work

  • Elbow tight to you, hand away from you.
  • It also sets up the arm triangle and armbar.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving space between your chest and their arm.
  • Cranking fast.

Drill it

5 slow reps each side.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Americana Submission · Side control
  • Arm triangle (head & arm) Submission · Mount

Set it up from

  • Side control: pins & pressure
  • Americana
  • North-south choke
  • Kimura trap from half guard bottom: sweep or back take
  • Kimura trap from side control to the back
  • Reverse triangle from top (side control or turtle)
  • North-south kimura to armbar
