Banana split from the truck

A leg-splitting submission from the truck where you push one leg away and pull the other in. Use it rarely, very slowly, and only where your gym allows it.

Back control → Submission

Step by step

  1. Get to the truck: sit behind and to the side of them with your legs locked around their near leg.
  2. Lie back onto your side and pull them back toward you so their hips stay trapped by your legs.
  3. Reach under their free far leg and hug the thigh behind the knee with both arms.
  4. Pull their far leg toward your head while you keep the lock on their near leg.
  5. Finish very slowly. Extend your locked legs to push their near leg away while you pull the far leg in, splitting their legs a little at a time. Release on the tap.

Details that make it work

  • The stretch comes from both directions at once: legs push one way, arms pull the other.
  • This is a groin and hip attack. Many people have no feeling of danger until it is too late, so go slower than you think.

Common mistakes

  • Pulling the far leg with your arms only while your locked legs stay bent.
  • Rolling or jerking into the split. Small, slow movements only.

Drill it

From the truck with a partner who knows the move. 6 entries each side to the leg grip with no stretch. Finish only very slowly, with a partner you trust.

If it fails, or what comes next
