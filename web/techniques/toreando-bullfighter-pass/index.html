Toreando (bullfighter) pass

A speed pass: control their knees, push their legs to one side, and run around to the other. Use it when they're on their back with their legs up.

Passing → Side control

Step by step

  1. Standing, grab both of their knees or shins.
  2. Push their legs to one side and pin them to the mat, arms straight.
  3. Step around in a circle to the other side, moving your hips past their feet.
  4. Drop your knee onto their belly or go chest to chest before they turn in.
  5. Settle into side control or knee on belly.

Details that make it work

  • Move your feet fast. This pass works on speed.
  • Pin their legs to the mat, not in the air.

Common mistakes

  • Pushing the legs then stopping, so they just recover guard.
  • Passing so close that they can grab you.

Drill it

5 rounds of 30 seconds, toreando in both directions.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Side control: pins & pressure Control · Side control

Set it up from

  • Posture up & open closed guard
  • X-guard entry and technical stand-up sweep
  • Single-leg X sweep
  • Ankle pick takedown
  • Inside position passing: hands and knees first
