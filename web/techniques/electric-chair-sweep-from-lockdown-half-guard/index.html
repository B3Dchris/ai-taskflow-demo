Electric chair sweep from lockdown half guard

A lockdown half guard sweep where you stretch their trapped leg and pull their far leg over your shoulder. Use it when they keep their weight back and resist the old-school sweep.

Half guard → Half guard top

Step by step

  1. From half guard bottom, lock your legs around their trapped leg in a lockdown and extend your legs to stretch it back. Their base breaks.
  2. Get the underhook on the trapped-leg side and slide your head and shoulders under them toward their far leg, as if going to deep half. Keep the lockdown on.
  3. Reach under their far thigh with your free arm and hug it. Pull their far knee onto your shoulder.
  4. Extend your lockdown to push their trapped leg away while you pull their far leg toward you. With both legs split, they cannot post and fall to their back on the far side.
  5. Release the lockdown, come up on your knees, and settle on top in half guard. Start passing.

Details that make it work

  • Stretch first, then reach for the far leg. Without the stretch they keep their weight on you.
  • Move to their far leg with your head under them. If you stay flat, you cannot reach it.

Common mistakes

  • Letting go of the lockdown before they are on their back.
  • Staying on the split to force a tap instead of coming up on top.

Drill it

Partner in your half guard at 50 percent. 3 rounds of 2 minutes: lockdown, stretch, far leg, sweep, come up on top, reset.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Half guard pass: flatten & free the leg Pass · Half guard top
  • Smash pass from half guard Pass · Half guard top
  • Banana split from the truck Submission · Back control
