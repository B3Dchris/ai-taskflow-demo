High-wrist guillotine

You choke with the bony blade of your wrist high on their throat. Use it from front headlock when they keep their head low after a snap-down or a failed shot.

Front headlock → Submission

Step by step

  1. From front headlock, wrap your choking arm around their neck so the bony blade of your wrist sits high across their throat.
  2. Reach your other arm over their far shoulder and grip your choking hand, palm on top.
  3. Point your choking elbow up and pull their head tight to your chest.
  4. Lift the wrist blade up into their throat, elbows tight to your ribs.
  5. Finish slowly on your knees, standing, or by sitting to guard. Never fall or jump onto them.

Details that make it work

  • The wrist bone does the choking, not your forearm.
  • Lift up into the throat. Do not bend their neck back.

Common mistakes

  • Gripping low on the forearm, which only squeezes the jaw.
  • Cranking the head instead of choking.

Drill it

10 slow grips and finishes each side from front headlock.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Guillotine (arm-in / high elbow) Submission · Front headlock
  • Anaconda choke from front headlock Submission · Front headlock
  • Go-behind from front headlock Back take · Front headlock

Set it up from

  • Flying guillotine
