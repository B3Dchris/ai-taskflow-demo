Half guard bottom: knee shield & underhook

The defensive base of half guard bottom: on your side, knee shield up, fighting for the underhook. Get this right and half guard becomes a safe place to work from.

Half guard → Half guard

Step by step

  1. Never lie flat. Turn onto your side facing them.
  2. Knee shield: your top knee across their belly, foot on their hip.
  3. Frame on their neck or biceps with your top arm.
  4. Fight for the underhook on the side of your bottom leg: dig your arm deep under their armpit.
  5. If they crossface you, frame and shrimp to get the knee shield back.

Details that make it work

  • The underhook decides half guard. Whoever wins it usually wins the position.
  • Flat on your back in half guard, you're about to be passed.

Common mistakes

  • Lying flat and letting them crossface.
  • Hugging their head instead of fighting for the underhook.

Drill it

Underhook fight from half guard: 3 × 1 min.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Underhook to dogfight to single leg Sweep · Half guard
  • Recover full guard from half Guard · Half guard

Set it up from

  • Side control escape: frame & re-guard
  • Elbow-knee escape (shrimp)
  • Knee cut defense: knee shield and underhook
  • Body lock pass defense
  • Kipping (heel-drag) mount escape
  • Guard retention against heavy pressure: frames and leg pummeling
