Leg lock defense

How to escape a leg entanglement: hide the heel, pull your knee past their hips, and get out. Tap early in training.

Legs attacked → Passing

Step by step

  1. Hide your heel from their arm: turn it away from the crook of their elbow. Against a straight ankle lock, flex your toes back toward your shin (boot the foot).
  2. Clear the knee line: pull your knee out past their hips.
  3. Push their legs away; post and stand if you can.
  4. Never rotate out of a locked heel hook. In training, tap early.

Details that make it work

  • The knee line decides leg lock exchanges.
  • Standing or stepping over breaks the entanglement.

Common mistakes

  • Rolling out of a heel hook (knee injury).
  • Panicking.

Drill it

Leg lock escape game: 3 × 1 min.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Knee cut (knee slice) pass Pass · Passing

Set it up from

  • Heel hook (know it, defend it)
  • Straight ankle lock defense
  • Kneebar defense
  • Kneebar from outside position (ashi)

This defends against

  • Ashi garami / single-leg X control
  • Heel hook (know it, defend it)
