Russian tie (two-on-one) to the back or single leg

A two-on-one arm control from standing that leads to a single leg or a go-behind. Use it against someone who reaches with their arm or hand-fights square in front of you.

Standing → Back control

Step by step

  1. From stance, grab one of their wrists with one hand.
  2. Bring your other arm across and clamp their upper arm just above the elbow, with your armpit over the top of their shoulder and your chest on their arm.
  3. Hip in tight beside them and pull the arm down so their weight sinks onto the near leg.
  4. If they pull back, drop your level and switch to a single leg on the near leg.
  5. If they push in or turn toward you, step behind them, release the arm, and lock your hands around their waist from behind.

Details that make it work

  • Your chest controls the arm, not just your hands. Stay glued to their shoulder.
  • Keep your hips beside theirs. If you stand square in front, they spin out.

Common mistakes

  • Holding the arm with straight arms at a distance.
  • Bending forward so they lift their head and turn into you.

Drill it

Partner in stance at 50 percent. 3 rounds of 2 minutes: Russian tie, then single leg or go-behind depending on their reaction.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Single leg (run the pipe) Takedown · Standing
  • Arm drag to body lock to mat return Takedown · Standing
  • Back control: seatbelt & hooks Control · Back control
