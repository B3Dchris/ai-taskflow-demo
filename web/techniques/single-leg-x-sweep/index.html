Single-leg X sweep

You trap one leg between yours, push their far hip, and lift the leg to drop them on their seat. Use it when you reach single-leg X from shin-to-shin or butterfly.

Seated guard → Passing

Step by step

  1. Get to single-leg X: their leg between yours, hugged to your chest, heel clamped by your arm.
  2. Your inside foot pushes their far hip. Your outside leg wraps behind their thigh, knee pointing out.
  3. Push their far hip away with your foot while your outside leg lifts their knee up toward their chest.
  4. They fall back to their seat. Keep hugging the leg.
  5. Post your free hand, technical stand-up, and step around their leg to pass. Or stay down and go to the straight ankle lock.

Details that make it work

  • Clamp the heel tight. If the foot slips out, the sweep is gone.
  • Push the far hip, not the thigh. The hip is what moves them.

Common mistakes

  • Pulling with your arms instead of extending with your legs.
  • Standing up with your hands off the leg, so they re-guard.

Drill it

10 sweeps each side, alternating finishes: 5 stand-ups and 5 straight ankle lock entries.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Straight ankle lock Submission · Leg entangle
  • Knee cut (knee slice) pass Pass · Passing
  • Toreando (bullfighter) pass Pass · Passing

Set it up from

  • De La Riva guard: hook and control
