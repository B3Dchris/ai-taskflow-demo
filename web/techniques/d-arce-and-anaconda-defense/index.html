D'Arce and anaconda defense

Defense against the D'Arce and anaconda chokes: stop the arm threading under your armpit and stay square. Use it in front headlock, turtle, or when passing with your head low.

Headlocked → Headlocked

Step by step

  1. Prevent it: keep your elbows tight to your ribs or knees so they can't thread an arm under your armpit and around your neck.
  2. If they thread the arm through, hand fight: grab the threading wrist and stop it reaching their other arm.
  3. Keep the trapped arm bent and that hand near your own face. The bent arm keeps the choke loose.
  4. Square up and keep your weight forward on them. Against the anaconda, post your free hand wide so they can't roll you onto your side.
  5. Once the grip breaks, pull your head out, get your feet under you and stand, or come up into their guard.
  6. Tap when the lock is closed and they squeeze or roll you. Both chokes come on fast.

Details that make it work

  • The arm under your armpit is the whole setup. Stop it there.
  • Weight forward and square. Getting turned onto your side finishes it.

Common mistakes

  • Reaching your arm forward on the mat, which feeds the thread.
  • Trying to pull your head out before breaking the grip.

Drill it

Partner in front headlock threads for the D'Arce at 50%: 5 hand fights each side, then 2 × 1 min positional.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Front headlock & guillotine defense Escape · Headlocked
  • D'Arce choke Submission · Front headlock
