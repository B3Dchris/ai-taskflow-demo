Japanese necktie from front headlock

A front headlock choke with a D'Arce-style arm, finished by dropping over their head and pulling your clasped hands in. Use it when they stay on their knees and block the D'Arce finish.

Front headlock → Submission

Step by step

  1. From the front headlock with them on their knees, sprawl your hips back and keep your chest heavy on the back of their head and shoulders.
  2. Thread your arm under their near armpit and under their neck, as for a D'Arce, so their head and near arm are inside your arms.
  3. Clasp your hands palm to palm instead of grabbing your biceps. Bring your top elbow down over the back of their neck.
  4. Drop your top shoulder to the mat past their head and lie across the back of their neck and shoulders, with your stomach covering the back of their head.
  5. Hook their near leg with your inside leg and pull it toward you so they cannot turn away.
  6. Finish slowly. Pull your clasped hands in to your chest and squeeze your elbows together while your hips come in a little at a time. Release on the tap.

Details that make it work

  • The pressure must come from your arm and their own shoulder on the sides of the neck. If you only feel the jaw or the back of the head, reset the arm.
  • Keep your weight on their upper back the whole time. If your chest comes off, they turn in and the choke opens.

Common mistakes

  • Driving your full bodyweight down onto the neck fast, which cranks the spine instead of choking.
  • Falling to the mat before your arm is deep, so the grip ends up on the jaw.

Drill it

Partner on knees at 50 percent. 3 rounds of 2 minutes: snap to front headlock, thread the arm, drop to the shoulder, finish slowly, reset. Switch sides each round.
