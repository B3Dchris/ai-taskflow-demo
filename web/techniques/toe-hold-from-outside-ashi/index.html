Toe hold from outside ashi

A figure four grip on the foot that turns it in toward their buttocks. Use it from outside ashi or single-leg X when they hide the heel, only where toe holds are allowed.

Leg entangle → Submission

Step by step

  1. From outside ashi or a single-leg X, keep their knee bent and pinned with your legs and their foot by your hip.
  2. Grab the top of their foot near the toes with the hand on the side of their little toe.
  3. Thread your other arm under their Achilles and grab your own wrist. This is a figure four on the foot.
  4. Keep their knee bent near 90 degrees and their leg pinned with your legs so it cannot straighten or turn.
  5. Finish slowly. Turn their toes down and in toward their buttocks with your figure four, a few degrees at a time. Release on the tap.

Details that make it work

  • The grip on the toes must be over the top of the foot, not the ankle.
  • Pin the leg first. If their knee is free, they turn with the lock and it fails.

Common mistakes

  • Letting their leg straighten, so the lock slides off.
  • Twisting fast. The foot and knee can tear before they feel pain.

Drill it

From outside ashi with a partner. 8 grips each side with no turn, then 4 slow finishes each side.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Straight ankle lock Submission · Leg entangle
  • Ashi garami / single-leg X control Control · Leg entangle
  • Kneebar from outside position (ashi) Submission · Leg entangle
