Body triangle escape from the back

How to escape back control when they lock a body triangle: neck first, then break the lock at the foot and turn in. Use it when their legs are locked around your waist from the back.

Back taken → Half guard top

Step by step

  1. Defend the choke first: chin down, both hands on the choking arm. Nothing else matters until your neck is safe.
  2. Find the lock: one of their legs crosses your belly and its foot is tucked behind their other knee.
  3. Keep one hand on the choking arm. With the other hand, or your free heel, push their tucked foot out from behind their knee.
  4. Slide your shoulders to the mat on the side of their choking arm as the lock loosens.
  5. Get your back flat on the mat, turn in toward them and come up in their half guard or on top.
  6. Tap if the choke gets under your chin, or the squeeze on your ribs stops you breathing.

Details that make it work

  • Neck first, legs second. People get choked while fighting the triangle.
  • Break the lock at the foot. That's where it's weakest.

Common mistakes

  • Both hands on their legs while their choking arm is free.
  • Trying to push the whole leg off your belly.

Drill it

Partner holds back control with a body triangle: 3 × 1 min. Reset when you break the lock and turn in.
