Sitting to guard safely

Sitting down to guard on your terms instead of getting taken down. In no-gi it's how most guard players start, but only when you sit close and with a grip.

Standing → Seated guard

Step by step

  1. Take a grip first (wrist or collar tie) so they can't rush you as you sit.
  2. Sit down onto your hip with your hand posted behind you, close to your butt, knees bent.
  3. Immediately put your feet on their hips or knees, or a shin between you.
  4. Keep your head off the mat, face them, and settle into seated or butterfly guard.

Details that make it work

  • Sit close enough to touch them. Sitting far away lets them pass with no contact.
  • Your post hand is also your technical stand-up if they back off.

Common mistakes

  • Flopping onto your back from a distance.
  • Sitting with your legs flat and no frames up.

Drill it

10 controlled sit-downs to seated guard, alternating sides.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Guard retention: frames & hip escape Guard · Seated guard
  • Butterfly (hook) sweep Sweep · Butterfly
  • Shin-to-shin to single-leg X Entry · Seated guard
  • Technical stand-up Escape · Seated guard
