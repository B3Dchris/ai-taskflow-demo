Stand-up from turtle

A wrestling stand-up from turtle with your back pressing into them while you fight their hands. Use it when you would rather be standing than fight from the bottom.

Turtled → Standing

Step by step

  1. From turtle with them behind you, lift your head and push your back up into their chest.
  2. Grab the hand around your waist with both hands so they cannot lock their grip.
  3. Post your outside foot flat on the mat, then the other, and stand up with your back pressing into them.
  4. Peel their hands off your waist, turning your hips away from their grip.
  5. Turn to face them with your elbows in and get back to your stance.

Details that make it work

  • Head up and back pressure first. With your head down, they keep you flat.
  • Fight their hands the whole time. If they lock their grip around your waist, they can lift and return you.

Common mistakes

  • Standing up with your back bent forward.
  • Turning to face them before their hands are off your waist.

Drill it

Partner rides your turtle at 50 percent. 6 stand-ups to stance, then 3 rounds of 1 minute where you only score by getting to stance.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Stance, motion & hand fighting Control · Standing
  • Single leg (run the pipe) Takedown · Standing
  • Double-leg takedown Takedown · Standing
