Blue belt · Escape · Core

Back escape

Defend the choke with your hands, then get your shoulders to the mat on the choking-arm side and turn in. Hands first, always.

Back taken → Half guard top

Step by step

  1. Defend the choke first: chin down, both hands on the choking (over) arm, pull it off your neck.
  2. Scoot your hips and slide your shoulders to the mat on the side of their choking arm.
  3. Pin their bottom leg and push the hook off your hip with your hand or foot.
  4. Get your back flat on the mat and turn in toward them.
  5. Come up in their half guard or guard, or on top.

Details that make it work

  • Hands first, then hips. If your hands aren't defending, you get choked.
  • Going to the choking-arm side means they can't easily climb to mount.

Common mistakes

  • Escaping toward their under-arm side.
  • Grabbing their hand and pulling straight down.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Half guard top: crossface & flatten Control · Half guard top
  • Posture up & open closed guard Pass · In closed guard

Set it up from

  • Rear-naked choke defense
  • Stopping the back take from turtle or a scramble
  • Body triangle escape from the back

This defends against

  • Back control: seatbelt & hooks
