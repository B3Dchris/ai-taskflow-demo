Side control: pins & pressure

The pin itself: how to stay heavy and stop them escaping once you're in side control. Holding the position is what lets you attack later.

Side control → Side control

Step by step

  1. Chest to chest, hips low.
  2. Crossface with your near arm, underhook their far arm.
  3. Near knee tight to their hip; other leg back for base.
  4. Hold with pressure, not tension: let your weight rest on them.
  5. When they frame or shrimp, follow their hips; switch your hips if needed.

Details that make it work

  • Their head is the steering wheel. The crossface keeps it turned away so they can't turn in.
  • Block their near hip with your knee to stop the shrimp.

Common mistakes

  • Hugging with your arms while your hips float.
  • Sitting too high on their chest, so they escape underneath.

Drill it

Positional: hold side control 1 min while partner escapes. 3 rounds each.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Side control to mount Control · Side control

Set it up from

  • Double-leg takedown
  • Single leg (run the pipe)
  • Wrestle-up from seated guard
  • Butterfly (hook) sweep
  • Knee cut (knee slice) pass
  • Body lock pass
  • Toreando (bullfighter) pass
  • Leg drag pass
  • Over-under pass
  • Underhook to dogfight to single leg

How to defend it

Side control escape: frame & re-guard: The main side control escape: build frames, bridge, shrimp away, and put your knee back in. The goal is getting your guard back, not getting on top.
