Butterfly (hook) sweep

The classic butterfly sweep: trap an arm, fall to that side, and lift with a hook. It works best when their weight is leaning forward onto you.

Butterfly → Mount

Step by step

  1. Sit up close, chest to chest, both feet hooked inside their thighs.
  2. Get an underhook on one side; with your other hand, trap their opposite arm at the triceps.
  3. Fall to the side of the arm you trapped, so they can't post with it.
  4. Lift with the hook on your underhook side, throwing their hips over.
  5. Follow them over, free your leg, and settle in mount or side control.

Details that make it work

  • Trap the arm on the side you fall to. If they can post, the sweep fails.
  • Get them leaning forward over you before you go.

Common mistakes

  • Falling flat on your back instead of onto your side.
  • Lifting with both hooks.

Drill it

10 each side, cooperative. Then positional: start in butterfly, sweep or get passed.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Mount: stay & climb Control · Mount
  • Side control: pins & pressure Control · Side control

Set it up from

  • Sitting to guard safely
  • Guard retention: frames & hip escape
