Wrist lock from closed guard against a posted hand

A wrist lock from closed guard against a hand posted flat on your chest. Use it when they post on you to keep posture, only where wrist locks are allowed.

Closed guard → Submission

Step by step

  1. In closed guard, wait for them to post a hand flat on your chest or stomach.
  2. Cover the back of that hand with both of your hands and pin it flat to your body. Keep their palm on you.
  3. Hold your guard closed and pull them in with your legs so their elbow stays bent.
  4. Keep their elbow pointing out to the side and their forearm angled toward your face.
  5. Finish very slowly. Curl your chest up toward their forearm, bending their wrist back, a few degrees at a time. Release on the tap.

Details that make it work

  • The hand must stay flat on your body. If the palm lifts, the lock is gone.
  • Small movement, big pressure. Wrist locks hurt quickly, so go slowly.

Common mistakes

  • Grabbing the fingers instead of covering the whole hand.
  • Sitting up fast and snapping the wrist.

Drill it

Partner posts a hand from inside your closed guard. 8 reps each side to the pinned hand, then 3 very slow finishes each side.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Kimura from closed guard Submission · Closed guard
  • Armbar from closed guard Submission · Closed guard
  • Hip bump sweep Sweep · Closed guard
