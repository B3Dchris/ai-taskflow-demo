Purple belt · Submission · Key

Mounted triangle

You trap one arm high and lock a triangle from on top of mount. Use it when they push on your hips or chest to escape mount.

Mount → Submission

Step by step

  1. Climb to high mount, knees in their armpits.
  2. When they push on you, pin one arm high, alongside their head.
  3. Slide your knee on that side up beside their head so the arm stays trapped.
  4. Lift their head with your hand and slide your other shin under their head.
  5. Lock your other leg over your foot, their trapped arm inside your legs.
  6. Sit back, pull the trapped arm across their neck, and squeeze your knees together slowly until they tap.

Details that make it work

  • The trapped arm must be inside your legs before you lock.
  • Sit tall. Leaning forward gives them room to bridge.

Common mistakes

  • Locking the figure-four with both arms outside, which is just a headlock.
  • Squeezing fast instead of adjusting the angle.

Drill it

10 slow reps each side from high mount.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Armbar from mount Submission · Mount
  • Arm triangle (head & arm) Submission · Mount

Set it up from

  • Pendulum (flower) sweep from closed guard

How to defend it

Mounted triangle defense: How to stop the triangle from mount: keep your arm off your face and bridge before they lock their feet. Use it when they climb high and isolate an arm.
