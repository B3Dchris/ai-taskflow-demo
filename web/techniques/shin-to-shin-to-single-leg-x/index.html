Shin-to-shin to single-leg X

A simple entry from seated guard: shin against their shin, grab the ankle, and pull yourself underneath into single-leg X. It's the main way into no-gi leg attacks.

Seated guard → Leg entangle

Step by step

  1. From seated guard, put your shin against the inside of their lead shin and grab that ankle.
  2. Sit in close; slide your other foot to their far hip or behind their knee.
  3. Lift with your shin while pulling the ankle, then bring your outside leg across their hip.
  4. Lock single-leg X: their foot under your armpit, inside foot on their hip, outside leg across their butt.
  5. Off-balance them to the mat, or come up to a takedown if they stay standing.

Details that make it work

  • Pull yourself under them before you lift. Distance kills this entry.
  • Hug the foot to your chest to keep the leg.

Common mistakes

  • Entering from too far away.
  • Letting their knee slip out past your hip.

Drill it

10 entries each side to single-leg X; partner lets you off-balance them.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Ashi garami / single-leg X control Control · Leg entangle
  • Straight ankle lock Submission · Leg entangle

Set it up from

  • Sitting to guard safely
  • Guard retention: frames & hip escape
