Straight ankle lock

Your forearm under their Achilles, arching to bend the foot. It's legal at white belt in most rule sets and a good first leg lock.

Leg entangle → Submission

Step by step

  1. From ashi garami, wrap your forearm under their Achilles, using the bony blade of your wrist.
  2. Grip your hands (or your other hand on their shin), their foot tucked under your armpit.
  3. Push your hips forward and arch your back to finish.
  4. Squeeze your knees to keep the leg.

Details that make it work

  • The Achilles sits on the bony part of your forearm.
  • Arch with your whole body, don't just pull with your arms.

Common mistakes

  • Gripping too low, on the foot.

Drill it

5 slow reps each side.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Heel hook (know it, defend it) Submission · Leg entangle

Set it up from

  • Shin-to-shin to single-leg X
  • Ashi garami / single-leg X control
  • Single-leg X sweep
  • Kneebar from outside position (ashi)
  • Saddle (inside sankaku) control: breaking the knee line
  • Counter leg lock: clear the knee line into your own attack
  • Peterson roll (sit-out) from turtle to leg entanglement
  • Imanari roll to leg entanglement
  • Backside 50/50 heel hook
  • Tripod sweep against a standing passer
