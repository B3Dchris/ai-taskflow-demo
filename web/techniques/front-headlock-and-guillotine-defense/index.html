Front headlock & guillotine defense

Protect your neck with your hands, rebuild your posture, and get your feet under you. Then re-shoot or sit to guard.

Headlocked → Standing

Step by step

  1. Hands first: grab the wrist of the arm around your neck and pull it off your throat.
  2. Head up: step your feet under your hips to restore posture.
  3. Against a guillotine, keep fighting the choking hand, chin tucked, and turn your head into their chest; don't pull straight back.
  4. Re-shoot when they lift, or sit to guard if you can't free your head.

Details that make it work

  • Your hands protect your neck before anything else.
  • Get your feet under you. On your knees you're stuck.

Common mistakes

  • Pulling your head straight back, which tightens the choke.
  • Going limp.

Drill it

Positional: start in front headlock, escape. 3 × 1 min.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Stance, motion & hand fighting Control · Standing

Set it up from

  • Guillotine defense: in their guard and standing
  • D'Arce and anaconda defense

This defends against

  • Snap-down to front headlock
