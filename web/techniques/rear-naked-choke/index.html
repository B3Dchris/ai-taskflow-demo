Blue belt · Submission · Core

Rear-naked choke

Your arm wraps their neck from behind and squeezes both sides. It's the most common finish in no-gi.

Back control → Submission

Step by step

  1. From the seatbelt, slide your over arm across their neck, elbow under the chin.
  2. Grab your other biceps; put that hand behind their head (or on your own shoulder).
  3. Squeeze your elbows together and expand your chest into their back.
  4. If they tuck their chin, win the hand fight first (two-on-one their defending hand) or switch to an arm attack.

Details that make it work

  • The crook of your elbow goes under the chin, centered on the neck.
  • Blade of your hand behind the head; don't shove the head forward.

Common mistakes

  • Forcing it over the jaw (that's a crank, not a choke).
  • Losing the hooks while you focus on the choke.

Drill it

5 slow reps each side; partner taps early.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Back control: seatbelt & hooks Control · Back control

Set it up from

  • Arm drag from butterfly to the back
  • Mount to back take
  • Back control: seatbelt & hooks
  • Back take from turtle
  • Arm-trap rear-naked choke
  • Truck entry from turtle to back take
  • Berimbolo from De La Riva to the back
  • Kimura trap from side control to the back

How to defend it

Rear-naked choke defense: How to stop the rear-naked choke: chin down and win the hand fight on the choking arm. Use it the moment your back is taken, before you try to escape.
