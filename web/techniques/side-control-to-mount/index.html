Side control to mount

Slide your knee across their belly to move from side control to mount. Mount is more dominant and opens up more attacks.

Side control → Mount

Step by step

  1. Block their near elbow, or push their near arm across their body.
  2. Slide your near knee across their belly to the far side of their hip.
  3. Keep your chest on their chest; bring your other leg over.
  4. Settle into mount with your knees tight to their hips.

Details that make it work

  • Hide your knee from their hands as you cross.
  • Stay chest-heavy until both knees are down.

Common mistakes

  • Sitting up while sliding the knee across.
  • Getting caught in half guard by sloppy knee placement.

Drill it

10 transitions side control to mount and back.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Mount: stay & climb Control · Mount
  • Armbar from mount Submission · Mount
  • Arm triangle (head & arm) Submission · Mount

Set it up from

  • Knee cut (knee slice) pass
  • Half guard pass: flatten & free the leg
  • Side control: pins & pressure
  • Knee on belly and far-side armbar
