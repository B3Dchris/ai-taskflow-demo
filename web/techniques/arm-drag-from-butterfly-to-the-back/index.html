Blue belt · Back take · Core

Arm drag from butterfly to the back

From butterfly you pull their arm across and slide behind them. It's the go-to back take when they post an arm or reach for you.

Butterfly → Back control

Step by step

  1. From seated butterfly, grab their wrist with your opposite hand (cross grip).
  2. Reach across with your other hand and grab their triceps.
  3. Pull the arm across your body while you scoot your hips out to the side and behind them.
  4. Get your chest on their back; reach over the far shoulder or around the far hip.
  5. Put in your near hook and climb to a seatbelt.

Details that make it work

  • Move your hips, not just their arm: you go to them.
  • Keep their arm pinned to your chest as you climb.

Common mistakes

  • Dragging while sitting still.
  • Losing the arm before your chest is on their back.

Drill it

10 reps each side, ending in a seatbelt.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Back control: seatbelt & hooks Control · Back control
  • Rear-naked choke Submission · Back control
