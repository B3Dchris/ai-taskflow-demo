Body lock pass defense

How to stop the body lock pass: frame and hip escape before their lock settles, then re-guard. Use it whenever they close the distance to hug your hips.

Seated guard → Seated guard

Step by step

  1. Prevent it: keep your feet on their hips and your knees between you. The body lock needs their chest close.
  2. If they close in, frame on their shoulders or collarbones before their hands lock behind your back.
  3. Hip escape away from the side they're stepping to, to make space for a knee.
  4. Slide a knee back inside and re-guard: feet on hips, or butterfly hooks.
  5. If they trap one leg, lock half guard on it and get your knee shield and underhook.

Details that make it work

  • Early beats strong. Once the lock settles, it's very hard to move.
  • Frames first, then the hips move.

Common mistakes

  • Lying flat and hugging them back.
  • Pushing with straight arms one at a time.

Drill it

Partner hunts the body lock at 50%: 3 × 1 min. Reset when they settle or you re-guard.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Guard retention: frames & hip escape Guard · Seated guard
  • Half guard bottom: knee shield & underhook Guard · Half guard
