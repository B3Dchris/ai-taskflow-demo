Recover full guard from half

Make space and put your knees back in to get full guard back. It's the safe choice when you can't win the underhook.

Half guard → Closed guard

Step by step

  1. Frame with your forearm on their neck or shoulder.
  2. Shrimp your hips out away from them to create space.
  3. Bring your free knee inside and onto their belly (knee shield).
  4. Use your feet to free your trapped leg, then wrap both legs around them.

Details that make it work

  • Make the space first, then bring the knees in.
  • Recovering full guard is a valid half guard game for a white belt.

Common mistakes

  • Trying to pull your leg out before you've made space.
  • Losing your frame.

Drill it

10 reps each side.

If it fails, or what comes next

  • Closed guard: break posture & control Control · Closed guard

Set it up from

  • Half guard bottom: knee shield & underhook
  • Elbow-knee escape (shrimp)
  • Knee cut defense: knee shield and underhook
  • Kipping (heel-drag) mount escape
